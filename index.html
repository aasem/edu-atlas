The Big Picture

The seven questions set against the six traditions, with the movement of the whole atlas above them. Every part of the chart opens onto the atlas: choose a cell to read how that tradition answers the question and go on to its texts, choose a question or a tradition to read its page, or follow one of the three routes, which walk through the chart as a story. On a small screen, scroll the chart sideways. Open the chart as an image.

The Texts at a Glance

The chart sums up each tradition; here every text appears on its own. Each tile is one text, placed in its tradition. Colour the tiles by any of the classifications used in the entries: the learner's nature, the motive relied on, where the end comes from, who is educable, who holds authority, or whether a text leans toward formation or transmission. Tap a tile to go to the text.
